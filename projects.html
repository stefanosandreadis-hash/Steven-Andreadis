Project: Design of the EMST Identity
Client: National Museum of Contemporary Art Athens, EMST
Type, year: Identity System, 2019

The National Museum of Contemporary Art Athens (EMST) is situated in the old FIX brewery, an Athenian landmark with distinct architectural and sociological significance. The new logo of the National Museum of Contemporary Art Athens is based on the characteristic parallelogram shape of the building, which was originally created by the architect Takis Zenetos. The identity aims to establish a clear voice of a contemporary museum in Athens where it was much needed and to be open to the diverse exhibitions and cultural events it hosts. Project: Visual identity of Eleusis 2023
Client: Eleusis 2023, European Capital of Culture
Type, year: Identity System, 2023

The word Eleusis derives from the verb ελεύθω=to come, to arrive. In the myth, Eleusis means the arrival of a significant event, which indicates the beginning of spring. It is the place of arrival, the coming, presence and revelation. The city’s rich heritage and today’s willingness to advance are reflected in ‘Iera Odos’, the route that started from Athens and ended in Eleusis, which in ancient times was taken by a procession celebrating the Eleusinian Mysteries. This route signals the transition, the emergence, the realization, the awakening, and the coming and it becomes the symbol of the “2023 EΛΕVΣΙΣ” identity. For the Eleusis 2023 identity, we used two fonts, GFS Didot and Helvetica Neue to visually highlight the connection between the ancient legacy and the contemporary city. Red, is used as the colour that indicates life through the arrival of Persephone, but also the factory funnel flames and the working-class movement that marked the modern industrialized era of the city. While the main red route is widely used, the visual identity of 2023 EΛΕVΣΙΣ has also been developed on the idea of the different routes that lead to the various places of interest that serve as event locations. Always in conjunction with the use of typography or photographs of the sites. The aim is to familiarise the audience with the concept and the rich cultural heritage of Eleusis. The visual identity has been applied to various means of communication e.g. the website, posters, merchandise etc. Studio: MNP Athens Creative Director: Katerina Papanagiotou Senior Graphic Designer: Stefanos Andreadis Graphic Designers Team: Laios Papazoglou, Danae Tzotzou, Korina Gallika Project Manager: Spyros Acheimastos My role spanned the project from beginning to end, from designing the identity system and print collateral to developing the city wayfinding, website, brand manual and 2023 Eleusis Report, while overseeing the implementation of the identity across all touchpoints.

Project: Manna Design Hotel
Client: Manna Arcadia
Type, year: Branding, 2024

Based on the deep human connection with nature and the history of Arcadia, the identity of Manna Arcadia aims to highlight the unique energy that emanates from this enchanting place. The identity is not limited to what the visitor can see, such as the horizon with the imposing mountain ranges and the tree tops. Instead, it seeks to capture and express the invisible but strongly felt energy that emerges from direct contact with the place and its space. There is also a reference to the building itself, a former sanatorium built in 1929, which for years served as a place of care and rehabilitation. The logo of Manna Arcadia symbolizes the vibration, the living energy of the space, in a symbolic representation of its very essence. Project: Efthimis Filippou, Screenwriter Website
Client: Efthimis Filippou
Type, year: Website, 2025

Screenwriter Efthymis Filippou's website transforms into a dynamic, living experience centred around the titles of his works, which take the spotlight as the core content. These words are displayed in Century Schoolbook font and positioned centrally with a clean, symmetrical aesthetic. As users hover over the titles, the letters expand and intermingle, forming an organic, flowing typographic composition. This animation mirrors the rhythm of a breathing, evolving organism, before gracefully returning to its original form. Complementing the visual experience, an audio playlist features readings of excerpts from his works. These recordings immerse visitors in the evocative atmosphere of his texts, offering a multidimensional and deeply engaging encounter with his creative world. Project: The Invisible Map
Client: Eleusis 2023, European Capital of Culture
Type, year: Website, 2025

The Invisible Map is a living map of a city’s collective memory, tracing the personal stories, experiences and historical events of its inhabitants onto the urban landscape. Through testimonies, photographs, objects and small public sculptures marking significant locations, it creates an intangible museum of memories — a growing constellation of stories that reveals the hidden history and identity of a place. Developed for Elefsina, the project celebrates the relationship between people, memory and the city, offering a model that can be adapted to other European cities. Project: Identity for Athenian Restaurant Anther
Client: Anther Restaurant
Type, year: Identity, 2024

Anther is located on the ground floor of Perianth Hotel, housed within a 1930s building characteristic of the modernist architecture of the interwar period. The identity emerges from the dialogue between two distinct design languages: the Art Deco character of the existing architecture and a contemporary visual approach developed specifically for Anther. The name refers to the anther, the part of a flower where pollen is produced. A small, central element that embodies the ideas of growth, nourishment and life. This concept forms the starting point of the visual system. The design language draws inspiration from the centre of the flower. The repetition and rhythm of the typography create a sense of movement and diffusion, evoking the anther’s life-giving force. The materiality of the space, brown wood, red stone and metallic copper, is translated into the visual language of the identity. Red emerges as the dominant colour, creating, in contrast with the black typography, a sense of tension and a dynamic visual presence. At the same time, the repetition of the name becomes a key graphic motif throughout the system. Project: ANIME Poster Exhibition
Client: AIAP, Italian Association of Visual Communication Design & European Design Awards
Type, year: Poster, 2024

The 2024 European Design Awards were held in Naples, Italy, hosted by AIAP, the Association of Visual Communication Design. To celebrate Naples’ unique spirit, the awards featured an exhibition on the cult of death at the Santa Maria delle Anime del Purgatorio ad Arco Museum. 15 creative agencies from around the world were invited to participate in the ANIME exhibition and create a poster with the task of graphically interpreting the symbolic and semantic relationship with the culturally varied meanings of death around the world.Our poster depicts the entrance to Hades at the Sanctuary of Demeter in Eleusis, Greece.
